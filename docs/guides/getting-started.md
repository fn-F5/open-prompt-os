# Getting Started with Open Prompt OS

> **Direct Answer:** Open Prompt OS is a full-lifecycle prompt engineering workbench that turns rough one-line ideas into structured, model-calibrated prompt specifications for text, image, and video models—with instant no-signup access, automated Prompt Autopsy diagnostics, image-to-prompt reverse engineering, and one-click dispatch to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

---

## 60-Second Quickstart

1. **Choose a Studio Mode:** Select **Text / Work** (writing, code, strategy), **Image** (product shots, editorial stills), or **Video** (24fps cinema motion, storyboards).
2. **Describe Your Intent:** Enter a rough goal (for example, `"Design a multi-region failover runbook for [Service_Name]"`).
3. **Add Guided Building Blocks:** Tap optional visual cards for **Goal**, **Audience**, **Constraints**, **Output Format**, **Persona**, **Tone**, and **Guardrails**—or apply a framework inspired by **CO-STAR**, **RISE**, **CARE**, or **Chain-of-Thought**.
4. **Pick Your Target AI Model:** Calibrate syntax and constraints for OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Imagen, FLUX, Midjourney, Veo, or Runway.
5. **Compile & Dispatch:** Click **Compile structured prompt**, inspect extracted `[Variables]`, and click **Open in ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini** or copy with one click.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use Open Prompt OS?
No. Visitors can compile, optimize, diagnose, and reverse-engineer prompts immediately without signing up, subject to daily free anonymous limits. Signing in unlocks persistent history, your private Library, public share links, and cross-device sync.

### How does One-Click Send work?
One-Click Send encodes your compiled prompt into direct launch URLs for ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini when within safe URL length limits, and automatically falls back to clipboard copy with confirmation for longer specifications.
